The meter shows nothing

Allow microphone access in iPhone Settings → Earshot. Another app recording at the same time (a call, a voice memo) can also block the meter.

How accurate is it?

iPhone microphones are not certified sound level meters. Earshot turns off the system's automatic gain so levels aren't flattened, but each model differs by a few decibels. For readings you can compare, hold a reference meter next to your iPhone and set the offset in Settings → Calibration. Very loud sounds above about 110 dB can exceed what the microphone can capture.

A, C or Z?

dBA matches how loud sound feels and is what hearing-safety limits use. dBC counts more of the bass, useful for music and engines. dBZ is unweighted.

What is the noise dose?

The share of a safe daily allowance you've used today. NIOSH allows 8 hours at 85 dBA and halves the time for every 3 dB louder; OSHA allows 8 hours at 90 dBA and halves it every 5 dB. Every meter session today counts. Pick the standard in Settings.

My purchase isn't showing

Open the paywall (History → Unlock) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
