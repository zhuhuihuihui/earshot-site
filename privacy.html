Privacy policy · effective 2026-11-03

Privacy

Hush does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account and no advertising.

Microphone

Hush uses the microphone only while the meter is on screen, to measure how loud it is. Audio is analysed in memory on your iPhone, turned into a decibel number, and discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your history

Each session's start time, length, average and maximum level are stored only on your iPhone, for up to 365 days, and are deleted when you delete the app or swipe a session away. A CSV export leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself.

Purchases

Hush Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Hush only learns whether Pro is active on the device.

Children

Hush is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
