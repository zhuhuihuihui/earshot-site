Privacy policy · effective with Earshot 1.2

Privacy

Earshot has no account and does not sell personal data. From version 1.2 the free version shows one banner ad, served by Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase for usage stats and remote settings. Earshot Pro removes the ad. This page lists what each one receives.

Ads (free version only)

The banner is served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google receives your device's IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interactions (views and taps). From the second launch, iOS asks whether Earshot may let Google track you across other companies' apps and websites. Only if you allow it does Google also receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads; if you don't, ads are not personalised. In regions that require it, Google's consent form asks first, and you can change that choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Ads are limited to general-audience content. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. No ads are shown to Earshot Pro users or in the China mainland App Store, and nothing ad-related runs for them.

Usage stats

From version 1.2, Earshot can send usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, for example that the meter was started with A weighting, how long a session lasted (in ranges), or that the Pro screen was shown and from where. Firebase also receives a Firebase app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version and coarse location derived from the IP address. Events never include audio, decibel readings tied to a place, names or contact details. They are used only to decide what to improve, never for ads. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Nothing is sent from the China mainland App Store.

Microphone

Earshot uses the microphone only while the meter is on screen, to measure how loud it is. Audio is analysed in memory on your iPhone, turned into a decibel number, and discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your history

Each session's start time, length, average and maximum level are stored only on your iPhone, for up to 365 days, and are deleted when you delete the app or swipe a session away. A CSV export leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself.

Purchases

Earshot Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Earshot only learns whether Pro is active on the device.

Children

Earshot is rated 4+. It is not directed at children, ads are limited to general-audience content, and it asks for no names, contact details or location.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
